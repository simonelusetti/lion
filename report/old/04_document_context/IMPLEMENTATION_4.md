# Document-context nuisance penalty: implementation

[Code](../../../lion/zfree/), [Hydra config](../../../conf/zfree/default.yaml). Forge stages: prepare → contexts → calibration → train → analyze → report. Results live in `results/zfree/`; previous Stage-1 reports are archived before this study starts.

**Data/models.** EWT UPOS, EWT dependency relations, GUM UPOS; the same five held-genre rotations per task as Stage 1. Native training sentences of training genres train; native validation sentences of those genres provide policy (a) validation; all splits of the next genre provide policy (b) validation; the held test genre supplies OOD evaluation. ID testing uses native test sentences of training genres. Frozen cached `bert-base-cased` block 12, first WordPiece, all labelled words, MLP 768→256→labels, dropout .1, AdamW lr .001 / weight decay .01, clipping 1, seeds 0/1/2. All rules share genre-balanced sentence batches and initialization/dropout draws.

**Context.** A token’s context is the mean cached vector over all words in other sentences of its document. Native partitions are processed separately, so training context never includes held-out sentences. Single-sentence documents are excluded from the penalty, retained in CE; coverage is reported. Per rotation, exact covariance eigendecomposition fits 64-dimensional PCA, token-weighted centering/scaling, and ridge regression on eligible labelled training tokens only. Label regressors concatenate focal-label one-hot, own-sentence label histogram and other-sentence document label histogram, with intercept. Ridge minimizes mean squared error plus .001 times squared coefficients; intercept is unpenalized. Evaluation context uses frozen training transforms. Gold labels enter only training penalties and retrospective diagnostics, never predictor inputs.

**Loss.** Apply log-softmax to logits and subtract each focal label’s batch mean log-probability vector. Cross-covariance uses ordinary centering and divisor n; the penalty is its squared Frobenius norm. Rz uses residualized context on covered tokens. Rg uses one-hot genre on all tokens. Rrand uses a fixed random derangement of eligible training documents, assigning each recipient token a uniformly sampled residual vector from its donor document. This donor mapping is fixed across classifier seeds and λ, independent of genres. R0 has no penalty. CE always uses every labelled word in the batch. No pairwise kernel or token cap is needed.

**Calibration/selection.** One rotation per task (answers for EWT, academic for GUM), seed 0: train R0 for 100 steps, then measure CE and all three raw penalties on 12 further balanced batches. Let each rule’s median CE/penalty ratio define a λ scale. Four equally log-spaced values use log-center halfway between the extreme scales and log-spacing equal to their range, at least one decade. Thus the middle two values bracket the rule scales; the outer values extend sensitivity. The same grid is used for every rule and rotation within a task. Actual weighted-penalty/CE ratios are saved. All rotations train R0 plus 12 candidates. Shared λ=0 is eligible for each rule.

Each trajectory trains at most 600 steps, validating every 50. Best checkpoints are saved independently for ID-validation and held-genre-validation macro-F1; each policy selects λ by that criterion, preferring smaller λ on ties. A trajectory stops after both criteria have gone three checks without improvement, after at least 150 steps and a complete balanced-sampler pass (600-step cap always applies). The policies share one trajectory but can select different checkpoints. There are 45 fits, 585 candidate trajectories, and two checkpoint-policy evaluations per trajectory.

**Genre diagnostic.** E3 class-weighted linear probes read log-probabilities and predict genre separately within each focal label. All candidates and both policies train in parallel. ID-test documents are cross-fitted with five genre-stratified folds for EWT and two for GUM, which has only two native test documents per genre. Separate native ID-validation documents select probe checkpoints over ten epochs. E3 minimum supports are 32/8/8 train/validation/test per label×genre, required in every fold; labels need at least two eligible genres. Balanced accuracy averages eligible genres within label, then labels equally. Probe support and skipped cells are retained. Genre labels affect these probes, the Rg comparator, rotation/batch construction and policy (b); the Rz penalty/residualizer themselves do not use them. This study is not a fully genre-free data-selection pipeline.

**Measurements/statistics.** Primary 1 is selected Rz−R0 within-label genre balanced accuracy. Primary 2 is selected Rz−R0/Rrand/Rg OOD accuracy. Primary 3 is Spearman correlation across every candidate’s Z-free violation and genre dependence, pooled across tasks and separately by task, for each checkpoint policy; tied ranks are averaged. OOD macro-F1, ID accuracy and all λ curves are descriptive. Violation is recomputed on whole ID-test predictions with test-label centering, frozen training residualization, and coverage exclusion. Residualization removes fitted linear label effects; it does not identify all remaining correlation as nuisance.

Paired differences share rotation/seed and aggregate rotations equally. Report seed SD and 200 document-block bootstrap intervals, conditional on fitted predictors/probes and λ choices. ID resampling is stratified by genre; OOD resampling is within its single held-out genre. Violation is recomputed from document sufficient statistics for each draw. Spearman pools every candidate/rotation/seed, with a paired document bootstrap; its seed SD uses separate per-seed correlations. Unsupported cells/bootstrap replicates remain explicit. No bootstrap refitting or large family of tests is added.

**Deliverables.** `RESULTS_4.md`, this implementation, compact CSVs, and accuracy–λ / genre-dependence–λ / violation–dependence figures. Wall-clock includes preprocessing, calibration, training, probes and report generation, excluding queued waiting. The explicit protocol adaptations are the calibration formula, ridge strength, donor-token matching, separate probe validation documents and two GUM folds. Stage 1 is preserved separately.
