# Stage 1: fixed invariance modes

This implements the cost revision of [the original protocol](original_request_3.md). Code: [pipeline](../../../lion/invariance/stage1.py), [training](../../../lion/invariance/stage1_train.py), [penalties](../../../lion/invariance/stage1_loss.py), [analysis](../../../lion/invariance/stage1_analyze.py). [Hydra config](../../../conf/invariance/stage1.yaml).

**Scope.** EWT UPOS, EWT dependency relations and GUM UPOS; R0 (unregularized), R1 (conditional MMD), R2 (marginal MMD). No R3–R5, routing or GUM discourse. All labelled words of sampled sentences use cached frozen `bert-base-cased`, block 12, first WordPiece. MLP 768→256→labels, ReLU, dropout .1; AdamW lr .001, weight decay .01, per-readout gradient clipping 1. Seeds 0/1/2 share initialization, sentence batches and dropout draws across conditions.

**Rotations.** EWT tests all five genres in alphabetical order; GUM tests academic, conversation, fiction, news, whow. Validation is the next genre in the complete alphabetical genre list. Other genres contribute native training sentences; validation/test genres contribute all native splits. In-domain testing uses native test sentences of training genres.

**Pilot λ.** One rotation per task: answers for EWT, academic for GUM. Fit R0 plus R1/R2 at λ=.1,.3,1,3,10. Select each mode’s λ from this grid including shared R0=0, using mean validation macro-F1 across three seeds, with lower λ breaking ties. Remaining rotations fit this fixed λ and its next higher neighbour, or the lower neighbour if the selection is 10. The pilot already supplies those candidates for its own rotation. Primary comparisons use the pilot-selected λ; the neighbour is a sensitivity check. There are 45 fits and at most 279 readouts, versus 4,860 in the superseded grid. No test score selects λ.

**Step budget.** Maximum 600 optimizer steps; validate every 50. Each readout saves its best validation macro-F1 checkpoint and stops after three checks without improvement, once at least 150 steps and one complete balanced sampler pass have elapsed (the 600-step cap always applies). Stopped readouts no longer contribute losses. Batches contain equal numbers of sentences per training genre: nominal size 64 rounded down to 63 for EWT, 52 for GUM. Smaller genres are reshuffled and oversampled; the sampler cycles until the step budget or early stopping ends the fit. Equal sentence shares do not imply equal token shares. Cross-entropy uses every labelled word in each batch.

**Penalty.** Biased RBF MMD² including diagonal terms. For marginal MMD, independently subsample at most 256 tokens per genre each batch. For conditional MMD, independently subsample at most 256 per genre×gold-label cell; cells below eight tokens are skipped. Sampling is without replacement, refreshed every batch and shared across readouts. Genre-pair discrepancies are summed, not averaged. Conditional label weights use their full, unsampled batch token shares; subsampling changes only MMD estimation. The detached median-positive-squared-distance bandwidth is estimated per readout from the capped, genre-stratified marginal batch sample and shared by that readout’s conditional cells. Equal-probability samples use bandwidth squared 1. `kernel=linear` is available as the requested fallback but the launched configuration uses RBF. Linear MMD is the squared distance between genre mean probability vectors, with the same pair/label weighting.

**Post-hoc groups and shift.** Training genres’ labelled native-training tokens define case-sensitive form frequency and label entropy. Frequency <5 is rare/unseen; otherwise entropy ≤.2 or ≤.5 nats defines unambiguous, and the remainder ambiguous. These thresholds never affect R1/R2 fitting. Label shift is equal-genre generalized Jensen–Shannon divergence of Bernoulli(label versus other) distributions. Training-token-weighted tertile cuts stratify test tokens by their gold labels for analysis only. Ties remain together, so strata may be empty; group shift averages label scores over training tokens. Unseen labels have no measurable training shift.

**Measurements.** Primary 1: R1−R2 OOD accuracy overall. Primary 2: the unambiguous group’s R1−R2 accuracy advantage minus the advantage in ambiguous/rare tokens combined, overall and within label-shift tertiles. Separate ambiguous and rare contrasts, group accuracy/macro-F1, and in-domain accuracy are descriptive. Both thresholds reuse the same predictions. Positive within-tertile interactions are H_annot-consistent; attenuation is compatible with H_shift, but an interval crossing zero does not establish equivalence. Comparison 1 alone distinguishes neither explanation.

Rotations aggregate equally. Differences pair seed/rotation. Seed SD follows rotation averaging; 200 paired document-block resamples within each test genre yield percentile intervals, conditional on fitted seeds and pilot choices. Unsupported rotations and valid-bootstrap counts are recorded per contrast. No per-cell hypothesis-test family is added.

**Artifacts.** Forge tracks prepare → pilot → train → analyze → report:

```bash
.venv/bin/python -B -m lion.invariance.stage1 runtime.workers=0 runtime.batch_size=64
```

Models, step histories, predictions, pilot selections and timing: `results/invariance_stage1/`. Completed pilot fits are reused for their rotation; completed fits are retained on resume. Total Stage-1 wall-clock starts at preparation and includes pilot/training/analysis/figures, excluding the abandoned full grid. Tables and figures: `report/tables/invariance/`, `report/figures/invariance/`; results: [RESULTS_3.md](RESULTS_3.md).
