# Context sensitivity across encoder layers

Frozen bert-base-cased. A head reads the mean WordPiece vector of the focal word at one layer (0 = embedding output, 12 = last block), or a learned softmax-weighted scalar mixture of all 13 layers. The existing baseline classifiers and the scorer of both datasets read **layer 12** (final hidden state; source configs `method.kind=layers, method.layer=12`). Selectors, cuts, test rows and the four cached fills per row, mask kind and fold are reused unchanged; no scorer or selector was trained.

Estimate 0.47 h; under six hours, so all 13 single-layer heads were run. Actual: estimate 0.4 min, train 5.0 min, measure 2.9 min, analyse 0.2 min; wall-clock 0.82 h.

S = KL(original ‖ perturbed), γ=.01 over native labels; flips change the argmax. Natural = cached MLM fills; masked = one [MASK] per dropped word, same dropped sets. Shift = 1 − cos at the head's layer (mixture: mixed vector), divided by the mean cosine distance of random word pairs (different sentences and forms) at that layer. Gain = mean S / mean normalized shift. Contextuality = mean normalized distance of each occurrence from its word type's test mean, types with ≥5 test occurrences. Values are mean ± SD over three seeds; single-layer shift and contextuality do not depend on the seed (SD 0). Row SEs and every non-tabled measure are in the CSVs. Draws and folds are averaged within row and never count as rows. * = fewer than 50 rows (excluded from ratios).

Layer 0 under natural fills: fills keep the focal pieces and all piece counts, so the focal vector is unchanged up to float rounding (max cosine shift 2.4e-7; mean KL ≈1e-6). Its natural S, flips and shift are rounding noise from the bf16 head, and its natural gain and matched ratio are shown as —. Masked versions move layer 0 only through position embeddings.

CoNLL O: the calibrated cut retains no context (ρ=0 in both folds), so the selector and random masks drop the same set (all context words) in every row. Masked versions are identical and every nearest dropped word is at distance 1; natural fills differ only by independent MLM draws. O does not contrast selector with random.
